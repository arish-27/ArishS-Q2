AetherGrid Robotics — Internal Company & Product Handbook (2026 Edition)

1. Company Overview & Offices
AetherGrid Robotics Pvt. Ltd. was founded in March 2021 by CEO Kavya Menon and CTO Rohan Kulkarni. The global headquarters is located in Indiranagar, Bengaluru, India, with a North American engineering hub in Austin, Texas, and a European logistics depot in Rotterdam, Netherlands. AetherGrid builds autonomous warehouse sorting robots and fleet orchestration software.

2. Core Product Line & Pricing
- Rover-S1 (Compact Shelf Picker): Payload capacity 35 kg, battery runtime 10 hours, top speed 2.4 m/s. List price is $14,500 per unit, or $490/month under the Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) 36-month plan.
- Titan-M4 (Heavy Pallet Mover): Payload capacity 450 kg, battery runtime 14 hours, LiDAR + stereo vision navigation. List price is $38,000 per unit, or $1,250/month on RaaS.
- FleetOS Enterprise License: Cloud and on-premise orchestration dashboard priced at $2,200/month per warehouse site (up to 50 robots). Additional robot seats cost $35/robot/month.

3. Warranty, Maintenance & SLA
All new Rover-S1 and Titan-M4 units include a standard 24-month hardware warranty covering motors, LiDAR sensors, and main compute boards. Batteries carry a separate 12-month or 1,500-cycle warranty. Extended 48-month coverage (AetherCare Plus) costs 12% of the hardware list price annually.
Enterprise SLA guarantees 99.95% FleetOS uptime and a 4-hour on-site technician dispatch in Bengaluru, Austin, and Rotterdam metro zones. Unscheduled downtime exceeding the SLA triggers a 10% monthly service credit.

4. Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy
Hardware purchases may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery if unopened or defective, subject to a 5% restocking fee unless the unit arrived damaged in transit. RaaS subscriptions require 60 days written notice for cancellation after the initial 12-month lock-in period; early termination incurs 25% of the remaining contract balance. Approved refunds are processed within 7 business days via wire transfer.

5. Customer Support & Escalation Hours
Live phone and voice-agent support operates Monday through Saturday, 07:00 to 22:00 IST (and 08:00 to 20:00 CST for North America). Critical P1 fleet-halt incidents are handled 24/7 via the emergency hotline (+91-80-4090-7788) with a 15-minute response target. Non-urgent tickets can be emailed to support@aethergrid.example.com.

6. Security & Compliance
FleetOS encrypts all telemetry in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256-GCM. AetherGrid Robotics achieved SOC 2 Type II certification in January 2025 and ISO/IEC 27001 certification in August 2025. On-premise deployments support air-gapped Docker registries and customer-managed KMS keys.

7. Partner Onboarding & API Limits
Certified integration partners receive sandbox credentials within 2 business days of signing the Partner NDA. Production REST API rate limits are 600 requests per minute per tenant key, and WebSocket telemetry streams support up to 100 concurrent robot connections per gateway.
